Digital Payments Fraud Monitoring Analysis Summary :
This dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of 1 million digital payment transactions, totaling $166.37 billion. While the overall fraud rate is low at 0.11%, the financial impact is significant, with $1.36 billion lost across 1,142 fraudulent transactions.
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Total Transactions: 1,000,000
Total Volume: $166.37 Billion
Fraudulent Transactions: 1,142
Fraud Amount: $1.36 Billion
Fraud Rate: 0.11%
Transaction & Fraud Insights
1. Transaction Volume by Type
The majority of transaction volume is driven by CASH_OUT ($69bn), followed by TRANSFER ($55bn) and CASH_IN ($39bn). Although "Payment" and "Debit" types exist, their financial footprint is minimal in comparison.
2. Fraud Concentration
Fraud is strictly concentrated in two transaction types:
CASH_OUT: 578 fraudulent transactions.
TRANSFER: 564 fraudulent transactions.
Other methods (CASH_IN, DEBIT, PAYMENT) show zero reported fraud, indicating that high-value outbound movements are the primary risk vectors.
3. Temporal Patterns (By Step)
Transaction Spikes: Total transaction volume peaks significantly around "Step 20" (51K) and "Step 45" (45K).
Fraud Volatility: Fraudulent activity is highly erratic. Significant spikes in fraud occur around Step 10, Step 35, and a major peak of 24 fraudulent transactions at Step 65. Even when total transaction volume drops to near zero (after Step 90), fraudulent attempts continue to occur.
Critical Observations
High Severity, Low Frequency: While only 0.11% of transactions are fraudulent, they account for nearly $1.2M per fraudulent event on average ($1.36bn / 1,142).
Risk Correlation: The "TRANSFER" and "CASH_OUT" types are the only categories experiencing fraud. This suggests a need for enhanced verification specifically for these "money-out" actions.
